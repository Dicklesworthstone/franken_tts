1 · Model

The playground runs the real model locally — nothing you type or record leaves this tab. First use downloads the quantized model (≈2.0 GB, resumable, cached in this browser) and verifies every byte against pinned SHA-256 digests.

2 · Voice

Cloning runs the speaker encoder locally on your recording; the recording itself is discarded after the 4 KB voice vector is computed. Clone only voices you have the right to use.

3 · Speak

Speed honesty: this build is single-threaded WASM and synthesizes at roughly 0.2–0.3× real time on a fast laptop — a five-second line takes twenty-odd seconds. The native CLI runs faster than real time; browser threads are the roadmap. Desktop browsers only: the engine needs ~2 GB of memory.
